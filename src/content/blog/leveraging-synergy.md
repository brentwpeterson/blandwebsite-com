In today's fast-paced digital landscape, businesses need to move fast. That is
why we wrote this post slowly, so you could keep up.

## 1. Align your stakeholders

Get everyone in a room. Agree that alignment matters. Schedule a follow-up to
discuss what you aligned on.

## 2. Break down silos

Silos are bad. We are not sure why, but every post like this one says so, and we
are nothing if not consistent.

## 3. Circle back

Whatever you were going to do, do not do it now. Circle back. Circling is a
shape, and shapes are a form of progress.

## 4. Move the needle

Find the needle. Move it. If you cannot find the needle, a haystack makes an
excellent slide background.

## 5. Leverage synergy

We saved the best for last. Leverage the synergy. Now you know everything we
know, which, to be clear, is this list.
