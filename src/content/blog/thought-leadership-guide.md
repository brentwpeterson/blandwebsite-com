Thought leadership is simple. You have a thought, and then you lead with it. If
you do not have a thought, borrow one. Nobody checks.

## Start with a hook

Open with a one-line paragraph.

Then another one.

Then reveal that you were humbled by a barista.

## Share your learnings

Never say "lessons." Say "learnings." It sounds like you went to a conference,
which, if anyone asks, you did.

## End with a question

What do you think? Agree? Let us know in the comments. We will not read them,
but the algorithm will, and the algorithm is the real audience.
